# Infotech IT Solutions Portfolio Website

A responsive, single-page portfolio website for:
- Website development
- Mobile application development
- Meta Ads and digital marketing

## Preview locally
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open `index.html` in a browser. An internet connection is needed for the Google Fonts.
3. Before publishing, open `script.js` and WhatsApp is already configured as `917249229837` (India country code included). Replace the Instagram URL in `script.js` with your real profile URL; the current link goes to Instagram home until you update it.

## Customize before launch
- Confirm the exact business name and spelling you want to use.
- Add your business email, phone number, social links and service area.
- Replace the illustrative work cards with real projects when available.
- Add privacy policy/terms if you use analytics, lead collection or ad tracking.
- Connect a domain and hosting provider to publish the site.

The contact form prepares an enquiry in WhatsApp; it does not store submissions in a database.


## 3D and animation
The site includes floating hero elements, animated orbit rings, scroll reveal effects, subtle pointer-based 3D tilt on desktop cards, and a floating WhatsApp contact button. Motion is reduced automatically for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

## Background effects
The page includes subtle floating blue/purple 3D-style background blocks. They are decorative, sit behind content, and respect reduced-motion accessibility settings.
